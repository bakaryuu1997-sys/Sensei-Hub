Why do some people always seem full of energy while others do not? Let's look at the biological "Hardware."

### A. Mitochondria – The ATP Power Plants
Mitochondria are the organelles in your cells responsible for producing **ATP** — the energy currency of the body. 
- In people with a strong "Inner Fire," the quantity and quality of Mitochondria are very high. 
- Conversely, a sedentary lifestyle and negative mindset weaken Mitochondria. This is like having a top-tier CPU but using a cheap, low-wattage **PSU** (Power Supply Unit) that can't handle the load.

### B. Core Energy vs. Peripheral Energy
- **Core Energy**: Comes from your purpose in life and core values. It is as durable as nuclear energy.
- **Peripheral Energy**: Comes from sugar, caffeine, or temporary excitement. It is like a cheap power bank; it runs out quickly and makes the device run hot.
- A Mindset Master knows how to draw energy from the Core to maintain long-term system stability.

### C. The Role of Serotonin and Norepinephrine
- **Serotonin**: Creates a sense of security and stability (**Voltage Stability**). When Serotonin is low, the system is always in an alarm state, consuming energy irrationally.
- **Norepinephrine**: Creates focus and alertness (**Current Flow**). Too much causes a circuit burn (Stress); too little causes stagnation (Depression).

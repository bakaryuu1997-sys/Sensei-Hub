- **Action**: Find 2-3 colors that best suit your skin tone and personality. 
- **Rule**: Clean, well-fitting (Fit) clothing is a prerequisite. A well-fitting outfit is equivalent to the command `padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px;` — it makes everything look polished immediately.
- **Mindset**: You represent a premium brand.

Don't fake a smile. Practice smiling with your eyes (**Duchenne Smile**).
- **Technique**: When smiling, think of something you are truly proud of or a successful node in the past.

### Step 3: The "Signature Scent" - Installing a Scent Cookie
- **Execution**: Choose a light, appropriate masculine/feminine perfume.

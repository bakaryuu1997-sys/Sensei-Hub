## 📝 6. QUICK QUIZ: Final Elite Challenge

**Q1:**
"Regarding this matter, we will consider it positively (前向きに), but **at this stage, we cannot give a commitment (確約しかねます - Kakuyaku-shikanemasu)**."
What is the partner's position?
- A. Will sign the contract 100%.
- B. Interested, but cannot say "Yes" at this point (a sign of reservation/pending).
- C. Stopped considering.
- D. Telling us to hurry up with the commitment.

> **Interpretation:**
> - **Correct Answer: B**. **"~Shikanemasu"** (cannot do) is a standard expression for conveying rejection or reservation in business.

**Q2:**
In a listening question, the boss said **"That's news to me" (それは初耳だね - Sore wa hatsumimi da ne)** to the subordinate's report. What is the problem inferred from this situation?
- A. The boss has bad hearing.
- B. Lack of reporting, contacting, and consulting (Hou-Ren-Sou), and there was a leak in information sharing.
- C. It's good news.
- D. It's the first time they've met.

> **Interpretation:**
> - **Correct Answer: B**. **"Hatsumimi"** (First time to hear) is an expression containing dissatisfaction when one was not informed of information they should have known.

**Q3:**
In a reading question, it says "Plan A is 1 million yen, Plan B is 1.2 million yen but includes after-service," and in the listening, it is said "I want to emphasize long-term maintenance." What is the correct answer?
- A. Plan A.
- B. Plan B (Linking maintenance = after-service).
- C. Neither is good.
- D. Repair it myself.

> **Interpretation:**
> - **Correct Answer: B**. Spotting "Paraphrasing" of vocabulary is a J1+ basic.

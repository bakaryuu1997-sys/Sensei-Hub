## ⚙️ UNDER THE HOOK: Grand Master Psychology & Japanese Culture

A leader who has reached this level embodies the concept of **"Fueki Ryuukou"** (Immutability and Fluidity) proposed by the haiku master Matsuo Basho in their management.

1. **"Fueki" (Immutable)**: Essential values that never change (Sincerity, Gratitude, Respect for people). This is the "root" of management.
2. **"Ryuukou" (Fluid/Trendy)**: Things that must be continuously changed to suit the times (Technology, Business models, Market strategies). These are the "branches and leaves" of management.
3. **"Shu-Ha-Ri"**:
   - **Shu (Protect)**: Faithfully following Keigo and etiquette (Phase 1).
   - **Ha (Break)**: Breaking the mold and negotiating flexibly (Phase 2 & 3).
   - **Ri (Leave)**: Moving away from established forms and establishing a unique style based on one's own philosophy (Day 95).

The courage to change what must be changed, and the **'Pride/Dignity'** (Kyouji) to protect what must not be changed. Overcoming this **'Antinomy'** (Niritsu-haihan), we will continue to grow sustainably alongside society. From this place today, we take a strong new first step toward an **'Infinite Future.'**"

---

### 🔍 Nuance Analysis

1. **"Milestone" (Fushime)**: Originally the node of a bamboo. Refers to an important division or turning point. A standard expression for ceremonies.
2. **"Settle/Rest" (Anjuu-suru)**: Being satisfied with the status quo and remaining in a state of tranquility. Often used as a word of warning.
3. **"Sublate/Synthesize" (Shiyou)**: Integrating two contradictory things and raising them to a higher dimension (Aufheben).
4. **"Dispassionate/Cool-headed" (Reitetsu)**: Seeing through to the essence of things calmly, without being swayed by emotions.
5. **"Pride/Dignity" (Kyouji)**: Strong pride in one's own abilities or beliefs. Represents the dignity of a leader.
6. **"Antinomy" (Niritsu-haihan)**: A state where two contradictory laws both have equally valid claims.

B. Speed is necessary, but we should refrain from proceeding in a rough and immature manner.

**Q3: What is the best expression for a strong determination never to back down in the face of difficulty?**
A. *Ganbarimasu*.
B. *Doryoku shimasu*.
C. *Futaiten no ketsui de nozomu* (Proceeding with indomitable determination).
D. *Maketakunai desu* (I don't want to lose).

---

### 🔑 Answers & Explanations:

1. **A. Fusion (Yuugou)**: A word representing positive integration, suitable for the J1+ level.
2. **B**: A combination of **"Sessoku"** (Fast but poor) and **"Shinshumu"** (Refrain/Be careful).
3. **C. Indomitable Determination**: Represents a strong spirit like a samurai who "does not retreat" (Futaiten).

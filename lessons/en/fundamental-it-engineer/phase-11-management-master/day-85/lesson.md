# 🏗️ 4. THE FINAL BATTLE: MOCK TEST 100 QUESTIONS

## PART 1: IT SERVICE MANAGEMENT & OPERATIONS (QUESTIONS 1-25)

**Question 1 [ITIL 4]:** Which term describes "a set of specialized organizational capabilities for enabling value for customers in the form of services"?
A. Service Management. B. Service Level Agreement. C. Service Offering. D. Service Provision.
*Answer: A.*

**Question 2 [ITIL 4]:** Which activity in the Service Value Chain focuses on creating a shared understanding of the vision and direction for all stakeholders?
A. Engage. B. Plan. C. Improve. D. Design & Transition.
*Answer: B.*

---

## PART 2: CORPORATE STRATEGY & GLOBAL MARKETING (QUESTIONS 26-50)

**Question 26 [SWOT]:** A company has strong core technology (Strength) but lacks capital for international expansion (Weakness). The market is trending towards using their technology (Opportunity). Which strategy is most appropriate according to the SWOT matrix?
A. S-O strategy. B. W-O strategy. C. S-T strategy. D. W-T strategy.
*Answer: B. (Using external opportunities to overcome internal weaknesses).*

---

## PART 3: FINANCIAL ENGINEERING & IT ROI (QUESTIONS 51-75)

**Question 51 [Investment Appraisal]:** Project A has an NPV of 100M Yen and Project B has an NPV of 50M Yen. If both are technically feasible, which should you choose?
A. Project B. B. Project A. C. The one with the shortest payback period. D. Need IRR info.
*Answer: B. (Principle: Highest NPV is best).*

## PART 4: LEGAL, COMPLIANCE & CYBER ETHICS (QUESTIONS 76-100)

**Question 76 [IP Law]:** In Japan, how long is a software's copyright protected after the author's death?
A. 20 years. B. 50 years. C. 70 years. D. Permanently.
*Answer: C.*

**Question 78 [Labor Law]:** Customer A directly gives orders to an engineer from Company B (Outsourcing). Which law does this violate?
A. Copyright Law. B. Information Security Law. C. Labor Dispatch Act. D. Commercial Law.
*Answer: C. (Gisou Ukeoi trap).*

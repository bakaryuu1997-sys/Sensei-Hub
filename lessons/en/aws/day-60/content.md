# ⚙️ UNDER THE HOOD: The 2026 Certification Path

To become a world-class BrSE by 2026, here is the certification roadmap you need to conquer:

### 1. AWS Certified AI Practitioner (New - 2026)
-   **Audience**: For everyone who wants to understand Generative AI on AWS.
-   **Focus**: Bedrock, basic Prompt Engineering, AI Ethics.

### 2. AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer - Associate (2026) (#(ML Engineer))
-   **Audience**: AI system implementation specialists.
-   **Focus**: LLM-Ops, SageMaker Pipelines, model fine-tuning, and Vector Databases. This is the "gold" certification for the new era BrSE.

### 3. AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Professional
-   **Audience**: System architecture "veterans."
-   **Focus**: Designing large-scale systems, Multi-region, High Availability, and Migration.

# ☁️ AWS IN PRACTICE / CAREER STRATEGY

**How to double your BrSE salary by 2026?**

1.  **AI-First Mindset**: Always ask, "Can this task be optimized using AI?". Don't just be a document translator; be an AI solution designer for clients.
2.  **Certification + Portfolio**: Having qualifications is good, but having real-world projects (like the Capstone you just did) on GitHub is better. Show clients what you have built.
3.  **Soft Skills (Japanese + Logic)**: The ability to explain complex AI algorithms in simple Japanese to non-tech clients is the most valuable skill of a BrSE.
4.  **Deep Tech**: Don't stop at the surface. Deeply understand how models work, how to optimize tokens, and data security.

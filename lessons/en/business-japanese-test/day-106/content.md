## 🪝 1. THE HOOK: The "Lies" of the Graph and the "Truth" of the Audio

You are currently looking at a complex document with two overlapping line graphs. One is "Market Share," and the other is "Trend of Advertising Expenses." The share is flat (**Yokobai**), but advertising expenses are surging (**Gekizou**).
The audio flows in:
**"The growth rate (Shinchou-ritsu) of our share is not catching up with the amount of advertising investment, and efficiency is continuing to deteriorate (Akka no itto wo tadoru)."**

For a J1+ elite, this situation is not just "data" but means a **"failure of strategy."**
The second half of BJT Section 2 goes beyond the level of reading a single graph and requires the ability to instantly organize the correlation between multiple data points (deviation, saturation, reversal) using only auditory information.

**Why do even N1-level learners get confused by "Advanced Data Analysis"?**
Because they are distracted by a single event (e.g., "what increased") and cannot fully process **"Secondary Information" (Secondary Info)** such as "ratio" and "efficiency" behind it.

## ⚙️ 3. UNDER THE HOOD: Vocabulary Expressing the "Dynamism" of Data

### 1. Advanced Nouns That Question the "Quality of Change"
In J1+-level conversations, it's not just about increase or decrease; the "quality" is questioned.
- **"Kairi" (Deviation/Gap)**: When expectation and reality are far apart.
- **"Houwa" (Saturation)**: A state where the market is full and no further growth can be expected.
- **"Shinchou-ritsu" (Growth Rate)**: The percentage by which something grows.

### 2. Phrases Expressing the "Turning Points" of Trends
- **"Migikata-sagari wo dassuru"**: To emerge from a downward trend (the decline stops and recovery begins).
- **"Nobi ga donka suru"**: Growth slows down.
- **"Isshin-ittai"**: To oscillate back and forth (alternating between good and bad).

### 3. Keyword Indicators
- **"Shijou senyuu-ritsu"**: Market share.
- **"Soneki bunkiten"**: Break-even point.

## 🎭 SIMULATED DIALOGUE: "Market Analysis" at a Strategic Meeting

### Scene: A meeting to decide whether to withdraw from or continue a new business
- **Director**: "Looking at this graph of sales trends for the new product, it seems a state of **'Isshin-ittai' (ebb and flow)** is continuing?"
- **You**: "Yes. At the time of launch, the **growth rate (Shinchou-ritsu)** exceeded 20%, but currently, due to the entry of competitors, the market is in a state of **saturation (Houwa)**."
- **Director**: "What about the profit margin?"
- **You**: "Unfortunately, we continue to fall below the **break-even point (Soneki bunkiten)**, and the **gap (Kairi)** between sales and cost is widening. Since there is no **prospect (medo)** of emerging from the downward trend, I propose a pivot in strategy."

**💡 Nuance Analysis:**
- **"Medo ga tatanai"**: There is no prospect of achieving the goal.
- **"Soneki bunkiten wo shitamawaru"**: To be in the red (deficit).

## 🧠 5. EXAM LOGIC: How to Listen to "Dynamic Graphs" in Section 2

An "Elite Checklist" for zero mistakes in BJT chart problems:

### ⚠️ Check 1: Substitution of Axes
There are cases where the horizontal axis of the graph is not "Month" but "Quarter," or the unit of the vertical axis is not "Thousands" but "Ten Thousands."
- **Strategy**: In the 5 seconds before the audio starts, always confirm the **"Definition of the X-Y Axes."**

### ⚠️ Check 2: "Forecast" vs. "Result"
The test might play on a time twist, where the audio speaks of "last year's results" and the graph shows "this year's forecast."
- **Strategy**: Distinguish clearly between endings like **"...no mikomi desu"** (Forecast/Prospect) and **"...to narimashita"** (Result).

### ⚠️ Check 3: "Intersection (Cross)" of Multiple Line Graphs
Concentrate on where two data points reversed or where the gap widened.
- Words like **"Gyakuten-shita"** (Reversed), **"Sa ga chijimatta"** (Gap narrowed), or **"Naranda"** (Became even) serve as coordinates for choosing the correct option.

## 📝 6. QUICK QUIZ: Day 106 - Advanced Data Analysis Elite Challenge

**Q1:**
There are Graph A (Sales) and Graph B (Profit). Which diagram fits the explanation: "Sales are flat (Yokobai), but the profit margin is steadily improving?"
- A. Both A and B are rising.
- B. A is horizontal, and B is rising.
- C. Both A and B are falling.
- D. A is rising, and B is horizontal.

**Q2:**
What shape of graph is predicted from the statement: "The market is saturated, and the growth rate is slowing down (Donka shite iru)"?
- A. Continuing to rise at a sharp angle.
- B. The angle of the rise is gradually becoming gentler, approaching horizontal.
- C. Making a V-shaped recovery.
- D. Falling vertically.

**Q3:**
When someone says, "We will take every measure to fill the **'Kairi' (gap)** with the target value," what is the current situation?
- A. We are greatly achieving the target.
- B. We haven't reached the target, and there is a difference.
- C. We have forgotten the target itself.
- D. There is no gap at all.

---

### 🔑 Answers & Explanations:

1. **B**: Sales (A) are **"Yokobai" (horizontal)**, and profit margin (B) is **"Improving" (rising)**.
2. **B**: **"Saturation" (Houwa)** and **"Slowing down" (Donka)** indicate a trend where growth stops and becomes flat.
3. **B**: **"Kairi" (Deviation/Gap)** refers to the difference between the ideal state (target) and reality.

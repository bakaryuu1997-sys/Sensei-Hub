## 🎭 2. ANALOGY: Reading – Scanning a Blueprint

Let's compare solving a reading question to "searching for an 'Emergency Exit' in a blueprint of a giant building."

- **The Walls (Background/Greetings)**: Parts you don't need to read. Set phrases like **"We are deeply grateful for your continued patronage"** are just "background colors" to an elite.
- **The Windows (Core Data)**: The information you should look for. **"Points of Change" (変更点 - Henkou-ten)**, **"Implementation Date" (実施日 - Jisshi-bi)**, **"Target Persons" (対象者 - Taishousha)**.
- **The Hidden Door (Footnotes)**: Where the correct answer is hidden. Small text in the corner of a table.

- **N1 Logic**: You walk step-by-step from the front door (reading intensively) to find the emergency exit. You run out of time.
- **J1+ Elite Logic**: You first view the whole drawing from above and pinpoint only the location where the "Emergency Exit Mark (the question's keyword)" exists.

A J1+ elite in BJT decides "what NOT to read" before moving their eyes.

## ⚙️ 3. UNDER THE HOOD: "Information Hierarchy" to Hack Reading

Use these "three scanning modes" to conquer the reading section:

### 1. Grasp Header and Footer in "0 Seconds"
Who wrote to whom, when, and for what purpose?
- **Members in "Cc:"**: If a boss is in the Cc, it strengthens the nuance of "reporting/approval."
- **"Subject" (件名 - Kenmei)**: If the subject says **"【Important】"** or **"【Urgent】"**, the dates or deadlines that follow will be the core of the correct answer.

### 2. The "Inverted Triangle" Law
Business documents are written with the important information first.
- **1st Paragraph**: Conclusion (What happened).
- **2nd Paragraph**: Details (Reason/Background).
- **3rd Paragraph**: Future response (Next Action).
- **BJT Strategy**: If the question asks about the "Future Schedule," skip the 1st paragraph and go straight to the 3rd.

### 3. Decoding the "Axis" of a Table
In chart problems, the most important things are not the numbers but the "Item Names (Labels)."
- Is it a **"Year-on-year comparison" (前年比 - Zennen-bi)** or a **"Goal Achievement Rate" (目標達成率 - Mokuhyou tassei-ritsu)**?
- **Elite Insight**: Check the units (%, number of units, or millions of yen) first. Misreading units is considered a "fatal mistake" at the J1+ level.

## 🧠 5. EXAM LOGIC: "Instant Kill" Logic for the Reading Section

### ⚠️ Trap 1: Timeline of an Email Chain
If 3 emails are listed, the latest email is the "final decision."
- **Pattern**: In the 1st email they say "Plan A," in the 2nd it changes to "Plan B," and in the 3rd it becomes "Actually, a modified version of Plan A."
- **Strategy**: Read from bottom to top (newest to oldest).

### ⚠️ Trap 2: Other than "Maximum" and "Minimum" in a Graph
BJT asks "Which is the 2nd highest?" or "Which has the largest 'Margin of Decrease' (減少幅 - Genshou-haba)?"
- **Strategy**: Don't just get distracted by max/min; focus on the "Slope (change)" of the graph.

### ⚠️ Trap 3: Definition of Technical Terms
If there is a definition sentence like **"...shall refer to..." (～とは、……を指すものとする)** in the text, that is the key to the answer.
- **Strategy**: Draw a box around that definition. You must answer according to the "local rules" within that text, not general common sense.

## 📝 6. QUICK QUIZ: Reading Elite Challenge

**Q1:**
"This campaign will **end as soon as the inventory is gone** (在庫がなくなり次第終了)."
When will the campaign end?
- A. On the day the campaign period ends.
- B. The moment all products are sold (inventory becomes zero).
- C. On the 1st of next month.
- D. When someone says they want to stop.

> **Interpretation:**
> - **Correct Answer: B**. **"~Shidai"** (as soon as...) indicates that the situation proceeds simultaneously with the fulfillment of the condition.

**Q2:**
"Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraph, **this shall not apply in cases of urgency** (緊急を要する場合はこの限りではない)."
What happens in case of urgency?
- A. You must strictly follow the rules of the preceding paragraph.
- B. You don't have to follow the rules of the preceding paragraph (it becomes an exception).
- C. Follow the rules even faster.
- D. Call the police.

> **Interpretation:**
> - **Correct Answer: B**. The combo of **"Notwithstanding"** (～にかかわらず) and **"This shall not apply"** (この限りではない) is the strongest exception clause.

**Q3:**
In a pie chart, **"Others" (その他)** accounts for 30%. What is your analysis as a J1+?
- A. "Others" is not important.
- B. It is necessary to investigate the contents of "Others" in detail (because 30% is a non-negligible ratio).
- C. The graph is wrong.
- D. Interpret it however you like.

> **Interpretation:**
> - **Correct Answer: B**. In business, if "Others" is huge, it's a sign of insufficient analysis.

# 📚 BJT Master Business Japanese (N1 Elite Edition) - Day 14
## 🎯 Theme: Instructions and Orders [Shiji - Meirei] - Confirming Intent and Precise Follow-up
### 📊 Target Level: J3 -> J2+ (Professional Operations)

---

## 🪝 1. THE HOOK: "Nodding" Does Not Mean "Understood"

Your boss calls you in and assigns a rather complex task. You listen intently, nodding continuously, and finish with a confident: **「わかりました。やります！」** [Yes, I understand. I'll do it!].

After 3 days, you submit the result. Your boss looks at it and bangs the table: "What on earth did you do? This is not what I meant!"
You are bewildered: "But, that day you said..."
Boss: "I said 'Consider option A', not 'Finalize option A'!"

Congratulations, you've just fallen into the trap of **「聞き流し」 (Kikinagashi - Listening but letting it flow away)**. In J1+ culture, nodding (Aizuchi) is only a show of respect, not a confirmation of understanding. If you don't perform **「復唱」 (Fukushou - Repetition)** and **「意図の確認」 (Ito no kakunin - Confirming intent)**, you are playing a game of chance with your career.

---

## 🎭 2. ANALOGY: The Map and the Destination

Receiving instructions from a boss is like the boss handing you a **Map** and pointing to a **Star (Destination)**.
- A person at N1 level: Takes the map and starts running immediately.
- A person at J1+ level: Spends an extra 5 minutes asking:
    - "Where is the starting line?"
    - "Why do we have to go there?" (Purpose)
    - "When is the latest we can arrive?" (Deadline)
    - "Are there any traps on the way?" (Risks)

If you don't clearly identify the "Destination" (the boss's intent), then no matter how fast you run (work hard), you are just getting lost. J1+ is someone who knows to stop and ask for directions before wasting energy running in the wrong direction.

---

## ⚙️ 3. UNDER THE HOOD: The "Technique" of Deep Listening

When a Japanese boss gives an order, it often hides an **「期待値」 (Kitaichi - Expected Value)**.

1. **Confirming Intent**: Always ask about the **「目的」 (Mokuteki - Purpose)**. Example: "Is this data for internal reporting or to be sent to a customer?". If for a customer, you must make beautiful slides; if for internal reporting, just a data-rich Excel file is enough.
2. **Priority (Yuusen Jun'i)**: Never accept a task without knowing how important it is compared to your old tasks. Ask: **「今受けている業務と、どちらを優先すべきでしょうか」** [Should I prioritize this or my existing work?].
3. **5W1H**: Who, When (Deadline), Where, What, Why, How. In the BJT, **Who** and **When** are the most common traps.

## 🎭 4. SIMULATED DIALOGUE: How to Receive Instructions

**Situation:** Manager Yamada assigns An to prepare materials for tomorrow's meeting.

**Manager Yamada:** Ms. An, can you prepare the materials for tomorrow afternoon's regular meeting?
**An:** Yes, certainly. **Just to be sure, let me confirm**, are the contents of the materials intended to be an updated version of last week's weekly report?
**Manager Yamada:** That's right. Also, replace the progress graphs for each department with the latest ones.
**An:** Understood. Updating the graphs. **By when** should I finish creating them? Is tomorrow morning in time?
**Manager Yamada:** I want to check them once first thing tomorrow morning, so it would be helpful if I could have them by email by the end of today's work hours.
**An:** By the end of today, then. Understood. **Let me repeat that**. For tomorrow's regular meeting, I will update last week's report and insert the latest graphs, and send them to you by the end of today.
**Manager Yamada:** Yes, I'm counting on you.

---

### 🔍 Nuance Analysis

1.  **"Nen no tame kakunin sasete itadakemasu ga"** (Just to be sure, let me confirm): The divine "insurance" phrase. It shows you are careful and don't work blindly.
2.  **"Kigen wa itsu made ni"** (By when is the deadline): This is the most important question. In Japanese business, **keeping the deadline** is the #1 priority for trust.
3.  **"Fukushou itashimasu"** (I will repeat): Repeating all requirements at the end of the exchange eliminates 100% of misunderstandings. This is a typical J1+ skill.
4.  **"Asa ichiban ni"** (First thing in the morning): Means "Immediately at the start of tomorrow morning." When a boss uses this, you must submit by tonight or extremely early tomorrow before the boss arrives at their desk.

## 🧠 5. EXAM LOGIC: The "Assignment" Problem in BJT Section 2

BJT exam questions often have a form where a boss gives 3-4 requirements to 2 different employees. Question: **"Who does what, by when?"**.

- **Trap 1: Changing instructions mid-way.** At first, the boss tells employee A to do it, but then discovers A is busy and switches to B. Listeners often "remember wrong" the first data point.
- **Trap 2: Attached conditions (If-Then).** "If it rains tomorrow, prepare extra umbrellas for guests." Question: "What is the employee's task?". The correct answer must contain the condition "If it rains."
- **Trap 3: Incorrect honorific logic.** Does the boss use honorifics with you (Wrong) or do you use honorifics with yourself (Extremely wrong). The BJT tests sensitivity to status within orders.

## 📝 6. QUICK QUIZ

**Question 1**: When a boss assigns a task while you are too busy, what is the most professional response?
- A) Say immediately: "I'm too busy, I can't do it."
- B) Accept it to please the boss, then submit late later.
- C) "Understood. However, I am currently also proceeding with Company A's project. Could you instruct me on which one to prioritize?"

**Question 2**: Which phrase is most polite for reconfirming a deadline?
- A) When is the deadline?
- B) **"Is there a deadline by when it should be done?"**

**Question 3**: Upon receiving instructions, you discover a more effective method than the boss's way. What should you say?
- A) Your way is wrong; it must be done this way.
- B) "I will proceed as you instructed, but if it's alright, there is also this method. Which would be better?"

> **Explanation:**
> - **Q1: C**. This is the technique of **Confirming Priority**.
> - **Q2: B**. Using **"gozaimasu deshou ka"** turns the question into a request for guidance.
> - **Q3: C**. You must respect the original instruction first (**shiji-doori ni**), then propose (**teian**).

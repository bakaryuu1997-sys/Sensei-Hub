# 📚 BJT Master Business Japanese (N1 Elite Edition) - Day 89

## 🪝 1. THE HOOK: What is the "Price" of Profit?

Your company has achieved record profits. However, behind the scenes, rumors are spreading that environmental destruction is progressing at the sources of raw material procurement and that local workers are being treated unfairly.
Investors are leaving, and talented young employees are submitting their resignations one after another. Criticism of "success built on exploitation" is flooding social media.
Even if there is numerical profit, you are losing the **"License to Operate" (Sonzoku no Kyoyou)** from society.

*"Profit is not the goal, but a reward given as a result of continuing to provide value to society."*

At the N1 level, you may be able to read pamphlets on environmental protection. However, a J1 Mastery Master knows how to turn **"ESG (Environment, Social, Governance)"** into a **"Sustainable Growth Engine"** for the company, rather than a mere cost or obligation.
Today, we will learn the "words of ethics" for being a company that coexists with society and is loved even 100 years from now.

---

## 🎭 2. ANALOGY: A Company is a "Giant Tree in the Forest," and ESG is the "Roots and Soil"

Think of the company as a single **"Giant Tree" (Kyoku)** standing tall in the forest.
- **Profit (Fruit)**: It is the visible achievement. Everyone wants this fruit.
- **Governance (Trunk)**: It is a strong structure that supports the tree from the inside so that it does not fall no matter how much the storm blows.
- **Environment & Social (Roots and Soil)**: It is the earth from which the tree sucks up nutrients. If the tree pollutes the soil and withers the surrounding plants just to grow itself, the soil will eventually become thin, and the giant tree itself will fall.

ESG Management is:
- **Environment (E)**: Keeping the earth rich.
- **Social (S)**: Harmonizing with the ecosystem of the entire forest (local communities and employees).
- **Governance (G)**: Disciplining yourself so that the tree grows straight.

A J1+ level leader is the "Guardian of the Forest" who not only harvests the fruits but also protects the soil so that the forest remains rich for generations to come.
Day 89's words are tools for defining the "Dignity" (Hinkaku) of the company and taking deep roots in the earth of society.

## ⚙️ 3. UNDER THE HOOD: Advanced Compatibility of Profit and Social Contribution

### 1. Creating Shared Value (CSV)
From "donations" to "solutions through business."
- **Social Issues into Business**: A business model where solving social problems directly leads to corporate profit.
- **Decoupling**: Separating economic growth from environmental burden.
- **Elite Logic**: The keyword is **"Inclusivity" (Houshu-sei).** Use the expression **"Management that leaves no one behind."**

### 2. Ethical Governance and Beyond Compliance
Obeying the law is not enough.
- **Ethics (Rinri-kan)**: A culture of asking yourself "is it socially correct?" rather than "is it okay if it doesn't break the law?"
- **Well-being**: Recognition that the happiness of employees is the source of creativity.
- **J1+ Technique**: Use the words **"Public Vessel (Kouki) of Management."** Noble management philosophy unique to Japan, that a company is entrusted from society, is required.

### 3. Sustainability Report and Transparency
The weight of words.
- **Non-financial Information**: How investors emphasize values other than numbers such as sales (e.g., degree of contribution to the environment).
- **Engagement**: Deep dialogue with stakeholders (interested parties).
- **Logic**: For a J1 Master, ESG is not a "decoration." It is the **"Ultimate Form of Risk Management"** and **"Responsibility to the Next Generation."**

## 🎭 4. SIMULATED DIALOGUE: Decision Making Over the Introduction of ESG Strategy

### Scene 1: Voices Concerning Cost Increase
- **Sales General Manager:** If we switch to environmentally friendly materials, the cost will skyrocket. While competitors continue to sell cheap, won't we be "Eliminated" (Touta) from the market if we only say "Beautiful Things" (Kireigoto)?

**💡 Nuance Analysis:**
- **"Beautiful Things" (Kirei-goto) [綺麗事]**: Beautiful words without substance (often used ironically).
- **"Be Eliminated" (Touta-sareru) [淘汰]**: Inappropriate things being removed.
- **"Skyrocket" (Hane-agaru)**: Rising rapidly.

### Scene 2: Refutation as a "Public Vessel of Management" by the Top (You)
- **CEO (You):** If we look at it from short-term gain or loss, that may be so. However, profit that disregards the environment or human rights is now regarded as "Dirty Profit" in the capital market. A company is a **"Public Vessel of Management"** and exists to enrich society. If we do not decide on this transformation now, we will be abandoned by both investors and customers within five years.

**💡 Nuance Analysis:**
- **"Disregard" (Keishi) [軽視]**: Looking down on; underestimating.
- **"Be abandoned" (Mi-hanasareru)**: Being ignored; no longer cared for.
- **"Dirty Profit" (Kegareta Rieki)**: Profit obtained through improper means (strong expression).

### Scene 3: Commitment to the Future
- **CEO:** We will thoroughly implement "Decarbonization" and "Human Rights Due Diligence" throughout the supply chain. This is not a cost, but an "investment" in the future. I am confident that promoting business with high ethics will result in creating the "world's most trusted brand."

**💡 Nuance Analysis:**
- **"Due Diligence"**: Proper duty of care, investigation.
- **"Thoroughly Implement" (Tettei suru)**: Not doing it halfway; doing it completely.
- **"I am confident" (Kakushin shite imasu)**: Strongly believing.

## 🧠 5. EXAM LOGIC: BJT J1 Mastery - Detecting "Greenwashing"

### ⚠️ Trap 1: Superficial "Philanthropy"
- **Trap**: An option that you just need to donate regardless of whether it is related to making a profit.
- **Elite Logic**: At the high level of BJT, **"Relevance to the Business"** is emphasized. The correct answer is an option that makes the business model itself sustainable.

### ⚠️ Trap 2: ESG Only for Image Up (Greenwashing)
- **Trap**: An option to appeal as an "eco-friendly company" only through advertisements even though the substance is missing.
- **Logic**: A Master looks at **"Effectiveness."** Choose an option that emphasizes specific numerical goals and the results of external audits.

### ⚠️ Trap 3: Sacrifice of Employees
- **Trap**: The idea that unpaid overtime for employees is unavoidable for the sake of the environment.
- **Logic**: An Elite Master also emphasizes "S (Social)." Options that respect the working environment and diversity of employees are appropriate.

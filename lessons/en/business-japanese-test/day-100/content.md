## ⚙️ 3. UNDER THE HOOD: Practical Case Study "Negotiation for Strategic Alliance"

### [Scenario]
You are in charge of strategy promotion at the IT giant "Zenith Solutions." You are currently negotiating a technical alliance with a mid-sized manufacturer, "Mirai Manufacturing."

#### Step 1: Listening (Section 1: Instant Response)
The person in charge at the partner company said:
**"Your proposal is attractive, but due to 'balance' (kaneai) with our existing partners, it is a situation where we cannot reply immediately."**

**💡 Judgment:**
Hearing this and ending with "Oh, I was refused" is an average level. A J1+ elite reads from the word **"Kaneai"** that "there are stakeholders whose interests need adjustment" and takes the next step: **"Then, shall we prepare additional materials to resolve those concerns?"**

#### Step 2: Listening & Reading (Section 2: Synchronization of Charts and Audio)
The presented graph shows data where Mirai Manufacturing's share has turned from a **"Downward trend"** (Migikata-sagari) to **"Flat"** (Yokobai) over the past three years.
The audio flows: **"We have finally 'hit bottom' (Soko wo uchi), and signs of a 'reversal' (Hanten) are beginning to be seen."**

**💡 Judgment:**
Identify the lowest point of the graph from the expression "hit bottom" and correctly recognize the slight increase trend from there.

#### Step 3: Reading (Section 3: Deciphering Document Intent)
An email received after the negotiation:
**"Thank you for your valuable time the other day; I offer my 'Deep Gratitude' (Shinsha). Regarding the alliance, we will 'consider it positively,' but first, we wish to 'submit' (kyou-suru) it to the development department's 'Approval Request' (Ringi), and we would be 'Extremely Happy' (Koujin) if you could send us a detailed spec sheet."**

**💡 Judgment:**
Understand from the word **"Kyou-suru"** (to provide/submit) that the next hurdle is "approval from the development department," and arrange to send the spec sheet promptly.

## 📝 6. QUICK QUIZ: Day 100 Commemorative Challenge

**Q1:**
If you are told "I will submit it to the internal **'Ringi'**" in a negotiation, what is the other party's situation?
- A. It has already been decided.
- B. It is OK at the person-in-charge level, but a formal approval procedure as an organization is required.
- C. Consideration has been discontinued.
- D. They will consult with an external consultant.

**Q2:**
You received a report: "The result was that we were **'left in the dust' (mizu wo akerareru)** by competitors." What is your company's situation?
- A. We won by a landslide.
- B. We are evenly matched.
- C. We are losing with a large gap.
- D. We are in a cooperative relationship.

**Q3:**
In an email, it says, "I offer my **'Shinsha'** for your time while you are busy." What does this "Shinsha" mean?
- A. Deep apology.
- B. Deep gratitude.
- C. Both are possible depending on the context (apology or gratitude).
- D. It's just a greeting and has no meaning.

---

### 🔑 Answers & Explanations:

1. **B**: **"Ringi"** is a decision-making system unique to Japan.
2. **C**: **"Mizu wo akeru"** means to create a large gap in a race, etc. Here it is passive, showing that a gap was created (you lost).
3. **C**: **"Shinsha" (深謝)** has both the meanings of "deep gratitude" and "deep apology." Judge by the context before and after the email.
